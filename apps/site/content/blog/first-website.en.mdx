Module 1 of the Builder track has one goal: by the end, your child has built and shipped a real personal website. No experience needed.

## One idea per lesson

Each lesson starts with a short video and a clear explanation. Then come the "try it" steps: small tasks in a code editor that runs right in the browser. The very first one asks your child to write a heading:

```html
<h1>Hello, world!</h1>
```

As soon as they type it, the step is checked automatically. If something is missing, a hint in their own language points them in the right direction, without giving the answer away.

## Building up, step by step

Over the module, each lesson adds one new idea:

1. Headings, paragraphs and lists
2. Links and images
3. Colours and fonts with CSS
4. Boxes, spacing and layout
5. Pages that look good on phones
6. A first button that does something, with JavaScript

<Callout>
  Every step earns XP, and a little practice each day keeps the streak going. It is designed to
  reward steady progress, not long hours in front of a screen.
</Callout>

## Shipping the project

The module ends with a project: your child plans, builds and ships their own personal page to their portfolio. The portfolio is private, and with Premium you can share it with family through a link.
