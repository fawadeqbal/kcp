The club wants to see a **dark mode**, but the current page must keep working.

1. Make a branch called `dark-mode` and switch to it.
2. In `style.css`, make the background **black** and the text white.
3. Commit it on the branch. Then try `git switch main` and `git switch dark-mode` and watch `style.css` change!

Finish on the `dark-mode` branch.
