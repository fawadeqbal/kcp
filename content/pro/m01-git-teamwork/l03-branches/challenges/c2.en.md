A teammate finished a footer on the `footer` branch. It's ready, so bring it into `main`.

1. Look around first: `git branch` lists the branches, and `git log --oneline footer` shows the footer branch's commits.
2. On `main`, merge the `footer` branch.
3. Check that `footer.html` is now in your folder, and run `git log --oneline`.
