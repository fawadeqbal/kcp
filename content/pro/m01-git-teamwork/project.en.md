Every team project starts with a plan. Build the **home page** of a project you'd like to make with a team — the kind of page a team puts at the top of its repository.

## Your page needs

1. A **name** for the project in the heading.
2. A **paragraph** that says what it does.
3. A **list** of at least three features.
4. A **numbered list** (`ol`) of at least three steps: how your team works together (branches, pull requests, reviews).
5. A **footer**, and a **background colour** in `style.css`.

Ship it when every requirement is ticked. In a hackathon, your team starts from a page like this one!
