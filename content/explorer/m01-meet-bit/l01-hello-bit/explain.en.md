title: Hello, Bit!
summary: Meet Bit the robot and give it your first instructions with blocks.
---

This is **Bit**, a little robot who loves adventures. Bit can't think on its own — it does exactly what you tell it, one step at a time. A list of instructions for a computer is called a **program**.

You write Bit's programs with **blocks**. Drag a block from the toolbox and snap it under **when ▶ Run is clicked**. Blocks that are snapped together run from top to bottom.

- **move right →** takes Bit one square to the right.
- Two **move right** blocks take Bit two squares.

Press **▶ Run** to watch Bit follow your program. If Bit goes the wrong way, change the blocks and run it again — trying, looking and fixing is what real programmers do all day.

### Try it

Guide Bit to the flag. When Bit gets there, press **Check my blocks**.
