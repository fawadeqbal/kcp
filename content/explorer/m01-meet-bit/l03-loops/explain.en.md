---
title: Loops save time
summary: Use "repeat" to do the same thing many times with just a few blocks.
---

Imagine a hallway ten squares long. You could snap ten **move right** blocks together… but programmers don't like doing the same thing twice. They use a **loop**.

The **repeat 3 times** block runs the blocks inside it again and again:

- **repeat 7 times**
  - **move right →**

That's just two blocks, and Bit walks seven squares! You can put more than one block inside a loop. To walk down a staircase, repeat _move right_ then _move down_.

Looking for the part that repeats is called finding a **pattern**. Spotting patterns makes your programs shorter and easier to read.
