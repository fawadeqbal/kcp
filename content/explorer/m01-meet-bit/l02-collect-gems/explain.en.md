---
title: Collect the gems
summary: 'Teach Bit a new action: picking up gems on the way.'
---

Bit loves shiny **gems**. When Bit stands on a gem, the **collect the gem** block picks it up. If there's no gem under Bit, nothing happens.

The order of your blocks matters. This program walks onto the gem first, and only then collects it:

1. **move right →**
2. **collect the gem**
3. **move right →**

If you swap blocks 1 and 2, Bit tries to collect before reaching the gem — and misses it. When a program doesn't do what you expected, you've found a **bug**. Finding and fixing bugs is called **debugging**.

### Try it

Collect every gem and finish on the flag.
