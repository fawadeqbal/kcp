Time to make a game of your own! In **Star catcher**, the player steers Bit around space with the arrow keys. Every star Bit catches is worth a point — and then the star zooms off somewhere new. How many can you catch in 30 seconds?

## Your game needs

1. A **start message**: when ▶ Run is clicked, Bit **says** something to the player.
2. **Four arrow keys**: one _when … key is pressed_ script for each direction, each moving Bit that way.
3. **Points**: when Bit touches the star, **change score by 1**.
4. **A star that moves**: after a point, **move the star somewhere new**.

## Make it yours

When the requirements are ticked, play your game and make it better: change the score for each star, say something new when Bit catches one — it's your game!

When you're happy with it, press **Ship it**. Your game goes into your **portfolio**, where your family can play it too.
