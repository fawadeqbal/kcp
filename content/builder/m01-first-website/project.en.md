Time to build something of your own! Make a web page about **you**: the things you love, a picture, and a link to a site you like. When every requirement is ticked, press **Ship it** — your page goes into your **portfolio**, where your family can see it.

## Your page needs

1. A big **heading** (`h1`) — it's already there. Change the words if you like.
2. A **paragraph** (`p`) that introduces you. Use your nickname, never your real name.
3. A **list** of at least three things you love (`ul` with `li` items).
4. A **picture** from `images/` — `beach.svg`, `mountains.svg`, `city.svg`, `park.svg` or `moon.svg` — with **alt** text.
5. A **link** (`a`) to a website you like.
6. In `style.css`: a **background colour** for the page and a **colour** for your heading.

Write all of these in `index.html` and `style.css`. `script.js` is only for extras: things it adds to the page don't count for the list.

## Want to go further?

- Style your list or your picture with CSS: try `border`, `padding` or `border-radius`.
- Open `script.js` and make your page react when someone clicks it.

Your work is saved as you type. You can ship again after changing things — your portfolio always shows the latest version.
