---
title: Add a picture
hints:
  add_img: Use the <img> tag. It doesn't need a closing tag.
  img_src: Choose the picture with src, for example src="images/beach.svg".
  img_alt: Describe the picture in words with alt, for example alt="Waves on a sunny beach".
---

Add a picture of your favourite place with the `img` tag. Pick one of our pictures — `images/beach.svg`, `images/mountains.svg`, `images/city.svg`, `images/park.svg` or `images/moon.svg` — and don't forget the `alt` text.
